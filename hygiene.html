Personal Hygiene

Personal hygiene means keeping our body clean and healthy. Good hygiene helps us stay healthy and prevents many diseases.

Important Personal Hygiene Habits

  • Wash your hands regularly with soap and clean water.
  • Brush your teeth twice a day.
  • Take a bath regularly and wear clean clothes.
  • Keep your nails clean and trimmed.
  • Keep your hair clean and well maintained.

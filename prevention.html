Disease Prevention

Good hygiene and healthy habits can help prevent many common diseases. Keeping ourselves and our surroundings clean is an important part of staying healthy.

Simple Ways to Prevent Diseases

  • Wash your hands regularly with soap and clean water.
  • Drink clean and safe water.
  • Keep your home and surroundings clean.
  • Cover your mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing.
  • Dispose of waste properly.
